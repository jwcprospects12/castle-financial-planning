Questions people often ask before we speak

What happens in the free consultation?

We get an understanding of your situation and what you want to achieve, and I answer your questions. We talk about how I can help, and you decide whether to go further. There is no charge and no obligation.

When would I have to pay?

Only if we both agree my recommendations are in your best interest and you then ask me to put them in place. Any fee is explained and agreed before any work starts. See our advice fees.

Do I need a minimum amount to invest?

No. What matters is whether advice can add real value to you. The first conversation helps us find that out.

What does “independent” mean?

I do not work for any product provider or financial institution. I work only for my clients and in their best interests.

Can we meet in person?

Yes. We have offices in Chelmsford, Bishop’s Stortford and London, or I can visit you at home.
